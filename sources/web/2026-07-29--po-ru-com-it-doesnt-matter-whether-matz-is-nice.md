    <h1 class="post-title p-name" itemprop="name headline" data-pagefind-meta="title">It doesn’t matter whether “Matz is nice”</h1><h2>This SWAN is cooked</h2><p class="post-meta">
      <time class="dt-published" datetime="2026-07-29T07:25:00+00:00" itemprop="datePublished">2026-07-29
      </time></p>
  </header>

  <div class="post-content e-content" itemprop="articleBody">
    <p>I’ve met Matz once, I think, at <a href="https://2010.euruko.org/">EuRuKo in Krakow in 2010</a>. We
chatted briefly in Japanese. He seemed nice, but also quiet and
reserved. At the time, he didn’t speak much English; perhaps he could speak
more and just wasn’t particularly confident, but he didn’t.</p>

<p>Yukihiro Matsumoto, aka Matz, is the creator and chief designer of the Ruby
programming language. He isn’t outspoken, he doesn’t make many pronouncements
outside the strictly technical arena, and he conducts most of his online life
in Japanese. That provides a space onto which to project anything, and that
gave us a motto of the Ruby programming community: MINASWAN (“Matz is nice and
so we are nice”).</p>

<p>I’m not saying that Matz isn’t nice, but the “Matz is nice” trope was only ever
projection, and in the end it doesn’t matter, because if we’re honest, we must
conclude that the Ruby community is not “nice”.</p>

<p>Two of the most obvious “not nice” but powerful people in Ruby are David
Heinemeier Hansson, aka DHH, and Tobias Lütke, and <a href="https://rubyonrails.org/world/2024/speakers/yukihiro-matz">Matz shares a
stage</a> with both of them.</p>

<p>Let’s take DHH first. He is the creator of and prime mover behind the <em>Ruby on
Rails</em> framework. Rails is Ruby’s success story, and represents the vast
majority of Ruby development and deployment. It has given me much of my paid
work for the past couple of decades.</p>

<p>DHH owns the <em>Ruby on Rails</em> trademark and is, in effect, the dictator for life
of the project.
He was a controversial figure in the early days of his Ruby fame.</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>He’s noisy, he’s reactionary, he’s anti-intellectual, he’s very sure that he
is right, and he enjoys being rude.</p>

  <footer><cite><a href="https://tomstu.art/the-dhh-problem"><em>The DHH Problem</em>, Tom Stuart, May 2014</a></cite></footer>
</blockquote>

<p>But who among us has not been an idiot in their youth? He seemed to grow up,
calm down, chill out, and for a time even espoused inclusive policies
and ideas.</p>

<p>More recently, however, he has pivoted his entire online personality to being
obdurately “anti-woke”, and started to publish pieces that are increasingly,
unarguably, white nationalist and far right in tone and content, lauding
Stephen Yaxley-Lennon dba Tommy Robinson, attacking immigrants and other
minorities, and generally revelling in reactionary bigotry.</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>“As I remember London” should be a wake-up call for everyone in the Ruby and
Rails communities. This is not a man who wants to keep going. This is a man
who romanticizes a past that was predominantly good for white men. This is a
man who has spent years railing against diversity, equity and inclusion and
who spreads anti-trans rhetoric. This is a man who is deeply afraid of
immigration changing countries’ cultures and “national identities”, despite
this kind of change being a constant for the whole of human history. This is
a man who is a white nationalist. And he is in sole control of Rails, this
framework we all love.</p>

  <footer><cite><a href="https://davidcel.is/articles/rails-needs-new-governance"><em>Rails Needs New Governance</em>, David Celis, September 2025</a></cite></footer>
</blockquote>

<blockquote>
  <p>Unfortunately it isn’t just migrants and non-white people that have come
under fire in DHH’s recent writing and social media posts. There have been
multiple examples of anti-trans rhetoric. There was the post where he
described an ad featuring a plus-sized Black women as “grotesque” and
celebrated the ads being replaced with ones featuring “blond babies” (what is
it about the baby’s blondness that is relevant I wonder?). There have been
the posts veering into puritanical Pronatalism, another favourite subject of
the nationalist right. Whether it’s his direct intention or not, there has
been a consistent theme of othering and stigmatising in his recent writing.</p>

  <footer><cite><a href="https://tekin.co.uk/2025/09/the-ruby-community-has-a-dhh-problem"><em>The Ruby community has a DHH problem</em>, Tekin Süleyman, September 2025</a></cite></footer>
</blockquote>

<p>In the past few weeks, DHH has gone much further, calling for “remigration”,
the euphemism for mass deportation of citizens used by the far right.</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Because the sacred cow here is not just that mass migration must stop going
forward. It’s also that millions who are already in Europe must go.
Remigration has gone from a fringe concept to the mainstream discourse in
record time on account of that realization.</p>

  <footer><cite><a href="https://world.hey.com/dhh/three-sacred-cows-that-must-die-so-europe-can-live-1afb203d" rel="nofollow noreferrer"><em>Three sacred cows that must die so Europe can live</em>, David Heinemeier Hansson, July 2026</a></cite></footer>
</blockquote>

<p>He has also, most recently, compared Roma people to wolves that need to be shot,
and then <a href="https://nitter.net/dhh/status/2081435006770249831">got very upset when an LLM refused to translate it</a> because
it was “dehumanizing towards an ethnic group”. You don’t gotta hand it to the
LLMs, but on this occasion, fair play.</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>When wolves get out of control, you shoot them. When gypsies take over public
spaces, you deport them. This isn’t hard, it isn’t cruel. It’s the basic
logic of self-preservation.</p>

  <footer><cite><a href="https://world.hey.com/dhh/wolves-sheep-and-gypsies-ba44af6a" rel="nofollow noreferrer"><em>Wolves, sheep, and gypsies</em>, David Heinemeier Hansson, July 2026</a></cite></footer>
</blockquote>

<p>As Emily Gorcenski pithily observed:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>just figured out what the HH in DHH stands for</p>

  <footer><cite><a href="https://bsky.app/profile/emily.gorcen.ski/post/3mrnw2wbauc2p">Post on Bluesky by Emily Gorcenski, July 2026</a></cite></footer>
</blockquote>

<p>Hansson is also a board member of Shopify, a company that provides hosted
online shops, and who you may know from 10,000 annoying podcast ads.</p>

<p>Shopify employs five of the ten foremost current human (we’ll get to that)
contributors to the Ruby language. It is the largest sponsor of the <a href="https://rubycentral.org/">Ruby Central</a>
organisation, and exercises a great deal of <em>de facto</em> control over the Ruby
ecosystem as a result. When Sidekiq withdrew their sponsorship after
Ruby Central announced DHH as a speaker at the final RailsConf – the final
RailsConf because Hansson, piqued at not being invited to a previous year, had
<a href="https://world.hey.com/dhh/we-ve-all-had-enough-of-this-nonsense-8545dd26" rel="nofollow noreferrer">set up his own rival Rails World conference</a> – <a href="https://joel.drapper.me/p/rubygems-takeover/">Shopify pushed Ruby Central
to take over full control of RubyGems</a> in a very murky series of
events.</p>

<p>Shopify’s technical and labour contribution to Ruby has been significant and
valuable; however, along with its financial contribution, this has given it a
great deal of power and control over the language.</p>

<p>Billionaire Tobias Lütke is the co-founder and CEO of Shopify. In 2022, against
the wishes of 46% of shareholders, he was granted a “founder’s share” giving
him <a href="https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/shopify-stock-split-vote-1.6480520">40% voting power</a>.</p>

<p>This week, he’s in the news for <a href="https://www.blogto.com/city/2026/07/ontario-billionaire-tobias-lutke-under-fire/">endorsing the
idea that rich people should get more electoral power</a>.</p>

<p>Shopify has long been infamous for <a href="https://www.vox.com/2017/2/8/14555636/shopify-not-kicking-breitbart-out">hosting the online store of far-right
publication Breitbart</a> (<a href="https://nitter.net/atRachelGilmore/status/1806386063625359684">along with other right wing publications and
vendors of Nazi memorabilia</a>). They have defended this as supporting
free speech, but there has always been a suspicion that there might be more
alignment than that. Shopify’s COO until 2025, Kaz Nejatian, was behind a
<a href="https://pressprogress.ca/shopify-executives-right-wing-media-website-rails-against-immigrants-while-defending-a-legally-designated-terrorist-group/">Canadian right-wing anti-immigrant website</a>.</p>

<p>In January 2025, shortly after Donald Trump’s second election win, <a href="https://corporateknights.com/issues/2025-04-spring-issue/how-shopify-went-from-canadas-tech-darling-to-anti-dei/">Shopify
terminated their support programme</a> for Indigenous entrepreneurs and
their <em>One Million Black Businesses</em> initiative, and then in February closed
the Slack channel that had allowed participants to stay in touch. The members
of the equitable commerce team left the company without explanation. Despite
being a Canadian (by naturalisation) running a Canadian company, Lütke seems to
be increasingly aligned with Trump.</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>you are right that there was stuff spreading <em>everywhere</em> so I guess the
initial assertion is true that there was a lot of woke stuff at Shopify, but
it was never sanctioned.</p>

  <footer><cite><a href="https://nitter.net/tobi/status/2055706194925203570">Post on X, the Everything App, by Tobias Lütke, May 2026</a></cite></footer>
</blockquote>

<p>Lütke doesn’t say anything nearly as incendiary as DHH, but the actions of the
company he controls tell their own story, and his posts on X, the Everything
App, document his turn to the right, along with all the crypto and AI stuff
you’d expect to go along with it.</p>

<p>He gave Hansson a seat on Shopify’s board long after the latter had started on his
anti-“DEI”, anti-immigrant theme. Lütke also recently <a href="https://nitter.net/dhh/status/2081959039500751034">reposted DHH writing
about “not caving to the woke mob”</a>.
That’s more than just tacit acceptance.</p>

<p>I hope <a href="https://mastodon.me.uk/@Floppy/116997766777309382">the move to fork Rails</a> succeeds, but while it’s necessary, I’m
not sure it’s sufficient while Ruby as a whole is substantially controlled by
the same people.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, what of Ruby? The second most prolific contributor to the Ruby
codebase in the last six months is Claude. Is this even worth salvaging?</p>
