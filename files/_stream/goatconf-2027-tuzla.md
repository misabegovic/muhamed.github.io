After [EuRuKo 2024](https://2024.euruko.org) in Sarajevo and [RubyConf Austria](https://www.rubyconf.at/) in Vienna, I am starting a third conference: [GoatConf](https://goatconf.com/), in Tuzla, Bosnia and Herzegovina, on 3–5 June 2027. It is free to attend, and [IPI Academy](https://goatconf.com/supporter-ipi-academy.html), where I studied, is the host.

**Why.** The goal fits in one sentence: get great minds together in an affordable environment, hang out, spend time together, learn, share experiences. Tuzla makes the affordable part easy. It is a cheap city, the food is very good, and the Pannonian Lakes, three salt lakes with a beach, sit in the middle of town.

**Who it is for.** People who build and sell software: engineers, designers, product people, founders. Ruby is home for me and for several of the speakers, and Rubyists are very welcome. So is every other language and stack: the more mixed the rooms, the better the hallway conversations.

**Built like Pi.** I like how [Pi](https://pi.dev/) works: a small core that others extend. GoatConf works the same way. I provide the core:

- Two core days, Thursday and Friday, with one-hour sessions in all seven IPI rooms at once.
- An hour off after each session, on Freedom Square, under five minutes away. The hallway track is the point, not the gap between talks.
- Saturday for the community: a day at the lakes, and a goat hunt through town that ends at the goat statue, in the spirit of the [treasure hunt](/brain/treasure-hunt-open-sourced/) I ran in Vienna for RubyConf Austria.

Everything else is an extension. Anyone can [propose an event](https://goatconf.com/add.html) around the conference: an evening meetup, a hike, a workshop, a hackathon. The first one is in: the [BH Professionals Meetup](https://goatconf.com/event-bh-professionals-meetup.html) on Saturday evening, hosted by the BH Futures Foundation. What I don't provide is just as clear: food and drink are on whoever eats, speakers cover their own travel, and GoatConf does not record the talks, though you are welcome to bring a camera or a podcast. [Supporters](https://goatconf.com/supporters.html) help online only and never buy a slot on the program.

**Why the goat.** When Austria-Hungary ruled Tuzla, it banned goats. One man hid his and kept it, word spread, and soon the whole town was buying his milk and cheese. People in Bosnia still say "the whole of Tuzla milked one goat". The goat was the symbol of EuRuKo 2024, the first Ruby conference in the country, and it carries over: one small thing that feeds a community.

**Who is speaking so far:** [Paul Campbell](https://goatconf.com/speaker-paul-campbell.html) (Tito), [Carmine Paolino](https://goatconf.com/speaker-carmine-paolino.html) (RubyLLM), [Dejan Menges](https://goatconf.com/speaker-dejan-menges.html) (Enola), [Amar Mahmutbegović](https://goatconf.com/speaker-amar-mahmutbegovic.html) (Semblie, from Tuzla), [Cristian Planas](https://goatconf.com/speaker-cristian-planas.html) (Rails Scales!) and [Christoph Lipautz](https://goatconf.com/speaker-christoph-lipautz.html) (Meister). The call for talks is open, and everyone who proposes hears back.

**How to come.** [Register on Luma](https://goatconf.com/register.html); I confirm each registration, usually within a few days. [Propose a talk](https://goatconf.com/talk.html), or add your own event. The whole conference is planned in a [public repository](https://github.com/misabegovic/goatconf), so an event can also arrive as a pull request.

See you in Tuzla.
