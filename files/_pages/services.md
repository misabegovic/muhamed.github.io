I help Rails and product engineering teams adopt AI-augmented development in a way that survives contact with production.

Most teams have already bought the tools. The gap is the working method: how you go from intent to shipped code with agents doing much of the typing, without losing architecture, review quality, or the team's understanding of its own system. That method is what I teach and set up.

## Workshop: AI-augmented development for Rails teams

A one or two day hands-on workshop for your engineering team, on your codebase where possible.

What we cover:

- A working loop from intent to shipped code: specs and decision records (MADR) as the contract between humans and agents.
- Setting up coding agents for a Rails codebase: context, skills, guardrails, and repository conventions that make agents productive instead of chaotic.
- Keeping architecture intact: declaring architectural laws and checking them in CI, so agent-written code cannot silently erode structure.
- Evaluating AI features you ship: prompt management, datasets, scoring, and observability.
- What to automate, what to keep human, and how to review agent output without rubber-stamping it.

You leave with a working setup in your own repository, not slideware.
