# Homepage surface brief

Scope: the muhamed.at homepage (`files/index.html`, layout `home`, include `home-page.html`, styles `assets/home.css`, copy in `files/_data/home.yml`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: hiring managers and engineering leaders buying help, weighted equally; both decide in minutes whether he is credible and how to reach him. Action: email info@muhamed.at. Proof: tools built, teams led, community standing, working in public. Constraints: no testimonials or logos until permission exists; keep /writing/, /brain/, /politika/; plain CSS; no em dashes; fonts self-hosted.

History: four ui.sh directions and the technical-drawing roll (seed 2b6b8f33) were built and rejected by the owner on 2026-09-28. The owner then chose to follow solo Rails consultants and Rails consultancies (speedshop.co, thoughtbot.com, testdouble.com).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A solo expert's practice presented with a consultancy's polish, carried by real proof: his face, real company names, real tools, real conferences. It refuses decoration standing in for evidence.

OWN-WORLD: White paper, near-black ink, one signal yellow (#ffd21f) for the primary action, the headline highlighter, link underlines and the closing band; one dark section matching the portrait's black. Schibsted Grotesk throughout, heavy weights for headings. Hairline dividers and 3px ink rules, 6px radius, one soft shadow on the portrait caption only.

STORY: The visitor sees who he is and what he offers, recognises companies, reads three services and how an engagement runs, checks selected work and the tools he builds, learns he founded RubyConf Austria, sees he writes, and emails him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with wordmark, five links and a yellow "Get in touch". Left, the positioning headline with "without losing control" highlighted, the lead, the yellow button plus "See how I can help", the availability line. Right, the portrait at 4:5 with a white caption card hanging off its lower left (name, role, RubyConf Austria founder).

FORM: Owner-chosen reference set (solo Rails consultant sites), outside the roll; seed key 2b6b8f33 was the rejected roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
